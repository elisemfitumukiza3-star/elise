Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

LearnLoop Rwanda ("LearnLoop", "we", "our" or "us") is an educational learning platform designed to help learners, educators and knowledge-sharing communities discover educational resources, take courses and share knowledge.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected when you use our website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

By using LearnLoop, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to read this policy. Where applicable law requires consent, we will seek it through the appropriate process.

2. Information We Collect

The information involved depends on the features you choose to use. It may include:

  • Account information: email address and information needed to create and manage your account.
  • Profile information: display name, biography, profile picture and other details you choose to provide.
  • Optional contact information: phone number and country calling code, where provided.
  • Community content: posts, comments, discussions, messages and other contributions you submit.
  • Uploaded materials: documents, images and other files you choose to upload.
  • Learning information: course progress, completed lessons, quiz results and certificate-related information.
  • Preferences: saved items, settings and certain information stored in your browser.
  • Technical information: limited technical information may be processed by the hosting and service providers supporting the website.

Please provide only information that is appropriate and necessary for your use of LearnLoop.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating, authenticating and managing learner accounts.
  • Providing educational resources, courses and learning features.
  • Displaying profiles and community contributions according to their settings.
  • Supporting discussions, messaging and community interactions.
  • Recording learning progress and supporting certificate issuance and verification.
  • Storing and displaying files that users choose to upload.
  • Maintaining platform functionality, safety and security.
  • Responding to questions, requests and support enquiries.
  • Improving the usability and reliability of LearnLoop.

4. How Information May Be Shared

LearnLoop does not make every piece of account information public. Information may be processed or shared in limited circumstances, including:

  • With other users: content and profile details you choose to make public may be visible to other users and visitors.
  • With service providers: providers that support hosting, authentication, databases and file storage may process information to provide their services.
  • For safety and legal reasons: information may be handled where reasonably necessary to address security concerns, enforce platform rules or meet applicable legal obligations.

We do not describe these providers as having permission to use your information for unrelated purposes. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply to services they operate.

5. YouTube API Services and Embedded Content

LearnLoop uses YouTube Data API Services to support the discovery of educational videos and may display selected videos through YouTube's embedded player.

When you use YouTube-related features:

  • Your search terms may be sent to YouTube to retrieve relevant video results.
  • Video information such as titles, thumbnails and other available metadata may be displayed.
  • When you play an embedded video, YouTube may process information through its player and related technologies.
  • Your use of YouTube content is also subject to Google's and YouTube's applicable terms and privacy practices.

LearnLoop does not claim ownership of third-party YouTube videos. We do not intend to download or re-upload YouTube videos as part of the video discovery feature.

Google and YouTube policies

6. Public and Private Information

Some LearnLoop features allow you to share information with other people. Posts, comments, public profile details and other contributions may be visible to users or visitors, depending on the feature and its settings.

Private messages are intended for the participants in the conversation. However, users should understand that no online communication system can guarantee absolute confidentiality.

Avoid publishing passwords, financial details, private contact information or other sensitive personal information in public areas of the platform.

7. Cookies and Browser Storage

LearnLoop may use browser storage, including local storage, to remember certain preferences, saved items and learning activity.

Your browser may also process cookies or similar technologies used by LearnLoop or third-party services. Embedded YouTube content may use technologies controlled by YouTube.

You can manage or clear browser storage and cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect features that depend on saved preferences or previous activity.

8. Data Security

LearnLoop uses third-party infrastructure and reasonable technical measures intended to protect information handled through the platform.

No website, storage system or method of transmitting information over the internet can guarantee complete security. Users should protect their account credentials and avoid sharing passwords with others.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained for as long as needed to provide the relevant features, maintain account and learning records, address support requests, protect platform security or meet applicable obligations.

If you want to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information associated with your LearnLoop account, contact us using the email address in the Contact Us section.

Please use the email address associated with your account where possible and explain what information or account you are referring to. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

We will review requests and explain if any information must be retained for a legitimate or applicable legal reason.

10. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to raise concerns about how it is handled.

You can also make choices about the profile information and content you submit to LearnLoop. Contact us if you need help with a privacy-related request.

11. Children and Minors

LearnLoop is an educational platform intended for learners and educational communities. Children and young people should use online services with appropriate guidance from a parent or legal guardian where required.

We do not intend this policy to replace any consent or protection required by applicable law. A parent or legal guardian who has a concern about a child's information may contact us using the details below.

12. Third-Party Providers and International Processing

LearnLoop currently uses the following services to support website operations:

  • Supabase: authentication, database services and file storage.
  • Vercel: website hosting and deployment infrastructure.
  • YouTube and Google: educational video search and embedded video playback.

These providers operate under their own service terms and privacy policies. Depending on their infrastructure and operations, information may be processed in locations outside the country where you access LearnLoop.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

LearnLoop may update this Privacy Policy as the platform, its features or applicable requirements develop.

The latest version will be published on this page. The effective date at the top will be updated when material changes are made. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
