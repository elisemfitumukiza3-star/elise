Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

LearnLoop Rwanda ("LearnLoop", "we", "our" or "us") is an educational learning platform that helps learners discover educational resources, take courses, share knowledge and connect with other learners.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users when using our website.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LearnLoop, we may collect:

  • Account information, including email address and account credentials.
  • Profile information, such as display name, biography and profile picture.
  • Optional phone number and country calling code.
  • Posts, comments, discussions and private messages you submit.
  • Files, images and other materials you upload.
  • Learning activity, course progress and certificate information.
  • Saved items, preferences and other information stored in your browser.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage learner accounts.
  • Provide educational resources and learning services.
  • Display profiles and community contributions.
  • Enable messaging and community interactions.
  • Track course progress and issue or verify certificates.
  • Maintain website security and improve LearnLoop.
  • Respond to questions and support requests.

3. Third-Party Services

LearnLoop uses third-party services to operate its features. These currently include:

  • Supabase: Used for authentication, database services and file storage.
  • YouTube Data API and YouTube: Used to search for educational videos and display selected videos through YouTube's embedded player.
  • Vercel: Used to host the LearnLoop website.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. When you use embedded YouTube content, YouTube may collect information according to Google's privacy practices.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy | YouTube Terms of Service

4. Public and Private Information

Information you choose to publish, such as posts, comments and profile details, may be visible to other LearnLoop users or visitors.

Private messages are intended for the participants in the conversation. Account information and private contact details are not intended to be displayed publicly.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in public posts or discussions.

5. Browser Storage

LearnLoop uses browser storage, including local storage, to remember certain preferences, saved items and learning progress. You can clear this information through your browser settings, although some features may no longer remember your previous activity.

6. Data Security

We use third-party infrastructure and reasonable technical measures intended to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained while an account is active or as needed to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes and meet applicable obligations.

If you want to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, contact us using the email address below. We will review and respond to requests subject to applicable requirements.

8. Your Choices

You can choose what information to include in your profile and public contributions. You may also contact us with questions or requests concerning your personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as LearnLoop develops. Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
